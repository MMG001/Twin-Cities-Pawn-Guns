Our Story

Twin Cities Pawn has been serving the Minneapolis–St. Paul area and its surrounding suburbs since 2010. As “Home of the 0% Pawn”, we pride ourselves in serving our customers and communities with honesty and integrity.

Come through our doors and you will find a clean, organized store with a friendly staff ready to help you. Whether you come in to pawn an item or you're looking to shop, we are here for you.

From one of the largest new and used firearm selections in the north metro to power tools, electronics, musical instruments, jewelry and gold — we buy, sell, trade and pawn quality goods every day, right here in Ramsey, Minnesota.
